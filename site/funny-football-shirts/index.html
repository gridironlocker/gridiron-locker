Funny Football Shirts

Funny football shirts do the hardest job in fan apparel: they have to land the joke and look right on game day. The designs below are the humor side of the locker - Sunday Funday rituals, Dawg Pound wit, cheesehead gags and the kind of mild sarcasm that gets a nod across a parking lot. All original fan artwork, printed on demand when you order.

The humor styles

Weekend ritual. Sunday Funday and Victory Monday designs name the weekly ceremony rather than a punchline - the joke is that everyone already knows it.

Dawg Pound wit. Cleveland humor runs loud and loyal: Beware Dawgs, Dawg Life, and the running argument the fanbase has with its own Sundays.

Game-day sarcasm. Defense-section chants, cheese-and-beer self-awareness, and rivalry-adjacent jabs that stay on the right side of the line - original phrases only, never protected marks.

Why these land better than a generic joke tee

A generic joke tee could belong to any fan of any team. These are built from the specific rituals our four fanbases actually have - the muni-lot energy, the cheesehead pride, the Texas swagger - which is why the same design reads as an inside joke in the right stadium parking lot and as ordinary humor everywhere else. Funny shirt FAQ

Are these shirts printed with team logos?

No. Every design is original fan artwork - team and city names appear as text, never as official marks, logos or player numbers. The full trademark notice explains the stance.

Is a funny shirt a good gift?

Humor is the most gifted category in fan apparel because it works off the field - watch parties, tailgates, office banter. Match the joke to the fan: Sunday Funday for the ritual crowd, Dawg Pound wit for Cleveland die-hards, cheesehead gags for Green Bay. Sizes and garment options are on each product page, and our gift guide picks by budget.

What sizes do the funny shirts come in?

Most designs print on tees in sizes S-5XL; a few run on hoodies, sweatshirts or mugs. Each product page lists exactly what that design offers. Measurements live in the size guide.

How long does a printed-to-order shirt take?

Printing starts after you order - there is no warehouse stock. Allow a few business days for production plus shipping; the checkout shows the live estimate for your address. Details on how ordering works.
