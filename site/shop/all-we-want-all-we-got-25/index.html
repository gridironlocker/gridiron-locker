The Idea Behind The Design

The brief for Cleveland Under Dawgs Playoffs Shirt was the phrase itself. THE CLEVELAND UNDER DAWGS - ALL WE GOT, ALL WE NEED - PLAYOFFS is short enough to print large on a t-shirt, which is why it works as apparel instead of a caption under a photograph.

On a t-shirt the type has to survive motion. That is why The Cleveland Under Dawgs - All We Got, All We Need - Playoffs is treated as a mark, not a paragraph - weight, spacing and contrast, in that order.

Cleveland fans measure the year in Sundays, not months. A piece carrying THE CLEVELAND UNDER DAWGS - ALL WE GOT, ALL WE NEED - PLAYOFFS is meant to sit in that weekly habit, not in a one-weekend costume. The crowd already has a chant for it: "Here We Go Brownies".

Game-day graphics have one job: be readable when the room is already loud. The Cleveland Under Dawgs - All We Got, All We Need - Playoffs is built for that noise, not for a lookbook.

The result belongs to Cleveland without needing a licence to prove it. Wear it, or give it, or leave it on a shelf until Sunday - the graphic will still be THE CLEVELAND UNDER DAWGS - ALL WE GOT, ALL WE NEED - PLAYOFFS.
