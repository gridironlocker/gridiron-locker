The Idea Behind The Design

The brief for Michigan M Vs All Tee was the phrase itself. M -VS- ALL is short enough to print large on a t-shirt, which is why it works as apparel instead of a caption under a photograph.

The print process shapes the drawing too. Everything is printed to order rather than screen-printed in bulk, so the artwork is prepared to hold its edges on the actual t-shirt and to keep its contrast across light and dark options.

Michigan vs Everybody is less a slogan than a weather report in Ann Arbor. Block lettering and a winged helmet silhouette do more work than a paragraph ever will. That is the football connection for this design - culture first, scoreboard second. The crowd already has a chant for it: "Go Blue".

Because it is fan-made rather than licensed, Michigan M Vs All Tee can carry M -VS- ALL without borrowing a mark it has no right to. You are buying the sentiment, not a replica.

The result belongs to Ann Arbor without needing a licence to prove it. Wear it, or give it, or leave it on a shelf until Sunday - the graphic will still be M -VS- ALL.

Why It Stands Out

This is a rivalry piece. M -VS- ALL picks a side in public, which is the whole job of a Michigan fan t-shirt. It is not trying to be polite, and it is not trying to look licensed.

Plenty of shirts mention Ann Arbor. Fewer of them are actually about M -VS- ALL.

Who It's For

Think people who hate loud graphics, first-time buyers, and Ann Arbor supporters who moved away and still set an alarm for kickoff. The artwork, M -VS- ALL with the Michigan M, travels because the idea is the city and the club, not a seating chart.

It also works as a present. There is nothing in the design that expires with a transfer, sizing is unisex, and it starts at $21.99.

Built for Game Day

Where this t-shirt actually gets worn: a draft-night group chat that has been arguing since May, a Sunday on the sofa when the weather makes the trip a bad idea, and a tailgate where you are standing outside for three hours before anyone throws a ball. It layers under a hoodie or a jacket once the temperature drops, which is most of the second half of the season. The graphic stays M -VS- ALL with the Michigan M in all of those rooms.

The t-shirt is made for normal use rather than a stadium costume, which is why most of the fans who buy one end up using it well outside Ann Arbor and well outside football season.
