The Idea Behind The Design

Every piece in this locker begins with something a Michigan supporter would actually say. Here the line is MICHIGAN -VS- EVERYBODY, and the drawing exists to keep it readable at a walk, in bad light, with a drink in the other hand.

On a sweatshirt the type has to survive motion. That is why Michigan -Vs- Everybody is treated as a mark, not a paragraph - weight, spacing and contrast, in that order.

Because it is fan-made rather than licensed, Michigan Vs Everybody Grey Sweatshirt can carry MICHIGAN -VS- EVERYBODY without borrowing a mark it has no right to. You are buying the sentiment, not a replica.

It is a small idea executed cleanly, which is generally what makes fan apparel get worn more than once a season. Michigan Vs Everybody Grey Sweatshirt is that kind of piece.
