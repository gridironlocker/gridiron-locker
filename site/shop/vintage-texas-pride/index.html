About This Design

Dallas Texas State Flag Pride Shirt is a hometown design for Dallas supporters. The artwork reads DALLAS TEXAS with state outline and Texas flag, printed as the single focal point of the t-shirt rather than one graphic among several.

It is a hometown mark more than a team mark - wearable in the stands and at the airport. Silver and blue reads as vintage the second you wash it twice.

Because it is fan-made rather than licensed, the design can say the thing supporters actually shout instead of the thing a brand is allowed to print. That is usually the difference between a shirt that gets worn and one that stays folded.

The Idea Behind The Design

Every piece in this collection starts with something a Dallas fan would actually say. Here it is DALLAS TEXAS, laid out so it survives being seen at a distance and in motion.

A design like this has to work at arm's length and across a room. That rules out fine line work and busy backgrounds, so the composition stays blunt on purpose: one message, strong contrast, and enough space around it that the fabric colour does the rest.

What you end up with is a t-shirt that says something specific about being a Dallas fan, rather than something generic about liking football.

Who It's For

This one is aimed at locals, hometown fans and anyone who has spent a season explaining their team to people who do not follow it.

Buying it for someone else is low risk - the t-shirt is unisex, the artwork is not tied to a single week of the season, and pricing starts at $21.99.

Game-Day Wear

Where this t-shirt actually gets worn: a tailgate where you are standing outside for three hours before anyone throws a ball, a sports bar two time zones from the stadium, and a draft-night group chat that has been arguing since May. It layers under a hoodie or a jacket once the temperature drops, which is most of the second half of the season.

Because the graphic is a fan statement rather than a licensed team mark, it does not look out of place away from the stadium either - which is the difference between a piece worn seventeen Sundays a year and one worn most weeks.
