The Idea Behind The Design

The brief for Bryce 19 was the phrase itself. BRYCE 19 is short enough to print large on a t-shirt, which is why it works as apparel instead of a caption under a photograph.

Fan merchandise has a practical constraint poster art does not: it is seen in passing, often in bad light, usually for about two seconds. The artwork is drawn for those two seconds - heavy shapes, generous spacing, no thin strokes that disappear on a dark t-shirt.

Ann Arbor Saturdays are maize, navy, and a crowd that treats a grudge as climate. Bryce 19 is drawn for that climate, which is why it does not need a licensed logo to feel local. The crowd already has a chant for it: "Go Blue".

Because it is fan-made rather than licensed, Bryce 19 can carry BRYCE 19 without borrowing a mark it has no right to. You are buying the sentiment, not a replica.

It is a small idea executed cleanly, which is generally what makes fan apparel get worn more than once a season. Bryce 19 is that kind of piece.

Why It Stands Out

Bryce 19 stands out because the artwork stays specific: Bryce 19, drawn for Michigan supporters instead of a generic football aisle. Independent fan art can say the line the crowd already uses.

If you wanted a replica, you would already have one. This is the other kind of t-shirt.

Who It's For

Think fantasy managers, diehard supporters, and Ann Arbor supporters who moved away and still set an alarm for kickoff. The artwork, Bryce 19, travels because the idea is the city and the club, not a seating chart.

It also works as a present. There is nothing in the design that expires with a transfer, sizing is unisex, and it starts at $21.00.

Built for Game Day

Where this t-shirt actually gets worn: the first cold road game of the year, a draft-night group chat that has been arguing since May, and a Sunday on the sofa when the weather makes the trip a bad idea. It layers under a hoodie or a jacket once the temperature drops, which is most of the second half of the season. The graphic stays Bryce 19 in all of those rooms.

It also survives the rest of the week. A fan-made graphic reads as a graphic first, so Bryce 19 works on a commute or a Saturday errand in a way a replica jersey does not.
