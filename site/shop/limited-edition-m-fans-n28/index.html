About This Design

Bet M Michigan Sweatshirt is an everyday fan design for Michigan supporters. The artwork shows BET M block lettering, printed as the single focal point of the sweatshirt rather than one graphic among several.

There is nothing fussy about it: one idea, balanced spacing, and colours that survive a wash cycle. Heavy navy crewnecks are the unofficial uniform of a Big Ten November.

It is an independent design, which is the point: you are not going to see it on four other people in the same section, and it does not carry a licensed logo it has no right to. What it carries is the sentiment Michigan fans already say out loud.

The Idea Behind The Design

The brief was a single graphic, not a caption: BET M block lettering, drawn to be recognised before it is examined.

The print process shapes the drawing too. Everything is printed to order rather than screen-printed in bulk, so the artwork is prepared to hold its edges on the actual surface and to keep its contrast across light and dark options.

It is a small idea executed cleanly, which is generally what makes fan apparel get worn more than once a season.

Who It's For

Think people who hate loud graphics, first-time buyers, and Ann Arbor supporters who moved away and still set an alarm for kickoff.

Buying it for someone else is low risk - the sweatshirt is unisex, the artwork is not tied to a single week of the season, and pricing starts at $36.99.

Game-Day Wear

Where this sweatshirt actually gets worn: a tailgate where you are standing outside for three hours before anyone throws a ball, a sports bar two time zones from the stadium, and kickoff at home with the game on and the phone face down. A crewneck sits between a tee and a jacket, which makes it the most-worn piece in most fans' rotations.

It also survives the rest of the week. A fan-made graphic reads as a graphic first, so it works on a commute or a Saturday errand in a way a replica jersey does not.
