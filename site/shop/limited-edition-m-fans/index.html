About This Design

Michigan Vs Everybody Navy Sweatshirt takes a single idea, MICHIGAN -VS- EVERYBODY with M logo, and builds the whole sweatshirt around it. It is an everyday fan design, not a team-shop reprint.

It is the low-risk pick in the collection - simple enough for a Tuesday, still clearly football on a Sunday. Michigan vs Everybody is less a slogan than a weather report in Ann Arbor.

Because it is fan-made rather than licensed, the design can say the thing supporters actually shout instead of the thing a brand is allowed to print. That is usually the difference between a shirt that gets worn and one that stays folded.

The Idea Behind The Design

The design began as a phrase rather than a picture. MICHIGAN -VS- EVERYBODY is short enough to set large, which is why it works on a sweatshirt instead of a poster.

The print process shapes the drawing too. Everything is printed to order rather than screen-printed in bulk, so the artwork is prepared to hold its edges on the actual surface and to keep its contrast across light and dark options.

It is a small idea executed cleanly, which is generally what makes fan apparel get worn more than once a season.

Who It's For

This one is aimed at people who hate loud graphics, everyday fans and anyone who has spent a season explaining their team to people who do not follow it.

Buying it for someone else is low risk - the sweatshirt is unisex, the artwork is not tied to a single week of the season, and pricing starts at $29.99.

Game-Day Wear

Where this sweatshirt actually gets worn: the walk from the parking lot to the gate in November, a watch party where half the room supports the other side, and a Sunday on the sofa when the weather makes the trip a bad idea. A crewneck sits between a tee and a jacket, which makes it the most-worn piece in most fans' rotations.

Because the graphic is a fan statement rather than a licensed team mark, it does not look out of place away from the stadium either - which is the difference between a piece worn seventeen Sundays a year and one worn most weeks.
