The Idea Behind The Design

SECOND ACT QB19 ANN ARBOR is the entire origin story of Second Act QB19 Ann Arbor Tee. Nobody asked for a second idea. The t-shirt is the first idea, held still long enough to print.

A design like this has to work at arm's length and across a room. That rules out fine line work and busy backgrounds, so the composition stays blunt on purpose: one message, strong contrast, and enough space around it that the fabric colour does the rest.

Ann Arbor Saturdays are maize, navy, and a crowd that treats a grudge as climate. Second Act Qb19 Ann Arbor is drawn for that climate, which is why it does not need a licensed logo to feel local. The crowd already has a chant for it: "Go Blue".

A licensed jersey says who is playing. This t-shirt says who you stood with. That is a different job, and it is why a name-and-number fan mark still works when it is not embroidered by a league shop.

It is a small idea executed cleanly, which is generally what makes fan apparel get worn more than once a season. Second Act QB19 Ann Arbor Tee is that kind of piece.
