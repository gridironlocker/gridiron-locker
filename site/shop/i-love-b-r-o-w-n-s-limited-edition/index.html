The Idea Behind The Design

I Love Browns Heart Sweatshirt starts as a drawing, not a caption. The artwork is I (heart) BROWNS, composed so it is recognised before it is examined - scale first, detail second, nothing competing with the read.

Fan merchandise has a practical constraint poster art does not: it is seen in passing, often in bad light, usually for about two seconds. The artwork is drawn for those two seconds - heavy shapes, generous spacing, no thin strokes that disappear on a dark sweatshirt. The heart mark is the soft version of a football shirt - still clearly Cleveland.

Cleveland football weather is Lake Erie weather - grey, loud, and not interested in fair-weather kits. I (heart) BROWNS is drawn for that climate, which is why it does not need a licensed logo to feel local. The crowd already has a chant for it: "Here We Go Brownies".

Independent artwork lets the sweatshirt say the thing Browns fans already say. That is usually the difference between a piece that gets worn and one that stays folded.

It is a small idea executed cleanly, which is generally what makes fan apparel get worn more than once a season. I Love Browns Heart Sweatshirt is that kind of piece.
