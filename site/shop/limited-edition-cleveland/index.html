The Idea Behind The Design

CLEVELAND is the entire origin story of Cleveland Bulldog Red Sweatshirt. Nobody asked for a second idea. The sweatshirt is the first idea, held still long enough to print.

The print process shapes the drawing too. Everything is printed to order rather than screen-printed in bulk, so the artwork is prepared to hold its edges on the actual sweatshirt and to keep its contrast across light and dark options. The bulldog is drawn as a character, so CLEVELAND with angry bulldog still reads from across a tailgate table.

The Dawg Pound has always been cheaper seats and a bigger mouth, which is the culture this collection draws from. The Dawg Pound has been the loudest cheap seats in football since the eighties. The graphic belongs to that room: Cleveland football, not a generic league aisle. The crowd already has a chant for it: "Here We Go Brownies".

The difference from a team-shop reprint is simple: this sweatshirt is one idea, CLEVELAND with angry bulldog, drawn for Browns supporters instead of a catalogue of approved icons.
