About This Design

Cleveland Helmet Phone Case is an everyday fan design for Browns supporters. The artwork shows Cleveland helmet on orange and black splatter, printed as the single focal point of the phone case rather than one graphic among several.

Cleveland fans measure the year in Sundays, not months. The restraint is the point. It is a design you can wear when you are not trying to make a statement.

Independent artwork gives it a different job from a replica jersey. A jersey says who is playing; this says who you are in the room - which is why it works well outside the stadium too.

The Idea Behind The Design

This one began as a drawing rather than a slogan. Cleveland helmet on orange and black splatter is simple enough to hold together at phone case scale, which is more than most illustrations manage.

Fan merchandise has a practical constraint that poster art does not: it is seen in passing, often in bad light, usually for about two seconds. The artwork is drawn for those two seconds - heavy shapes, generous spacing, no thin strokes that disappear on a dark background.

What you end up with is a phone case that says something specific about being a Browns fan, rather than something generic about liking football.

Who It's For

This one is aimed at everyday fans, first-time buyers and anyone who has spent a season explaining their team to people who do not follow it.

Buying it for someone else is low risk - there is no sizing decision, the artwork is not tied to a single week of the season, and pricing starts at $19.99.

Game-Day Wear

Where this phone case actually gets used: a tailgate where you are standing outside for three hours before anyone throws a ball, a sports bar two time zones from the stadium, and the walk from the parking lot to the gate in November. It is the fan detail that goes everywhere the rest of your gear does not.

It also survives the rest of the week. A fan-made graphic reads as a graphic first, so it works on a commute or a Saturday errand in a way a replica jersey does not.
