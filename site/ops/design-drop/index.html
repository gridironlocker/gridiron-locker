Week 1 drop — Sept 5–13, 2026

Eight slots, two per collection, from DESIGN-BLUEPRINT.md §5b. This is the art package: what each slot says, how it is set, what file it exports as, and what has to be true before it goes on the store. Statuses live here and on the operator board — never on a public page. A slot that is still brief shows a live sibling design on the shop, so nobody shopping ever meets an empty grid.

The Watson rule. W1-01 and W1-02 are the Cleveland quarterback slots. Slogan only: LET IT RIP and NEW ERA. No face, no surname, no #4, no number of any kind. If art arrives with any of those on it, it does not ship — not to the store, not to social, not to the board's live column.

Upload checklist

  1. Pass the law. No face, no surname, no number, no team mark. Watson slots: slogan only.
  2. Export the master. 4500 × 5400, 300 dpi, transparent PNG, sRGB, plus the SVG.
  3. Check it on both extremes. Lightest and darkest garment the slot will be sold on.
  4. Upload to the right Viralstyle collection and enable the garments in the slot brief.
  5. Re-crawl. python3 scrape_products.py then python3 dl.py.
  6. Write the copy facts. One entry in src/catalog.py: name, art, kw, theme (slogan-led wherever possible).
  7. Rebuild. python3 src/build.py — regenerates the Week 1 guide, sitemap and llms.txt.
  8. Flip the slot to live in src/build.py → WEEK1_SLATE, then python3 ops/board/build.py.
  9. Refresh the public page and confirm the grid renders full — no empty slots, no "coming soon", no "no design yet".

QA gate

Full list: DESIGN-BLUEPRINT.md §7. Short version — the slogan is in the bank, the palette is the collection's accent plus one ink, the file is the right size, the slug has no name or number in it, and the public page shows a live sibling.
