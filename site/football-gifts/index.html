Gifting FAQ

What makes a good football gift?

Match the gift to the fan, not the standings: a Browns fan laughs at Dawg Pound wit, a Michigan fan wears the rivalry, a Packers fan takes the cheese jokes. If you know their team but not their humor, the vintage-style classic designs are the safest bet. The budget gift guide has the longer version.

Can you deliver by a specific game or holiday date?

No promises - and anyone who guarantees a date on printed-to-order gear is guessing. Printing starts after the order and shipping time depends on the address; the partner's checkout shows the live estimate. Order as early as you can and the odds are firmly in your favor.

What size should I gift?

Each product page lists the sizes that design actually offers - most run S-5XL on unisex tees. When in doubt between two sizes, size up: relaxed fits better as a gift. The size guide has garment measurements.

Are these officially licensed team products?

No. Everything here is original, fan-created artwork made by fans for fans - team and city names appear as text, never official marks. The full story is in the trademark notice.
