Browns gift FAQ

What size should I buy as a gift?

Most designs print on unisex tees in sizes S-5XL - each product page lists exactly what that design offers. If the fan is between sizes, size up: relaxed reads better as a gift. The size guide has garment measurements.

Is Browns gear like this officially licensed?

No - and that is the point. Everything here is original, fan-created artwork: team and city names appear as text, never official marks. The trademark notice explains the stance.

When should I order for the holidays?

Everything is printed after the order, so order as early as you can - nobody can honestly guarantee a delivery date on print-on-demand gear. The partner checkout shows the live shipping estimate for the address.
