Michigan Vs Everybody Shirts: The Phrase Explained

Every fanbase has one phrase that needs no explanation inside the room and all the explanation outside it. For Michigan it is two words. This is what fans mean when they say Vs Everybody - and the designs in the locker that carry it.

What Vs Everybody means

The phrase names a mood, not a matchup: one team against everyone else, proudly. It is why the same three words show up in searches in every possible spelling - vs everybody, against everybody, vs the world - and why they work on a shirt long after any single game is forgotten. A chant celebrates what happened; Vs Everybody announces who you are.

Vs Everybody shirts FAQ

What does Vs Everybody mean to Michigan fans?

It is the us-against-the-world phrase: one team, everyone else, no middle ground. Fans say it before big games, after bad calls and on shirts all year - and unlike a score or a roster, the phrase never goes out of date.

Are these shirts officially licensed?

No. Gridiron Locker is an independent, fan-made store. Designs are original artwork that uses team and city words descriptively to say who a design is for. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or licensed by any league, club or university.

Which design should I pick - M vs Everybody or Michigan v Everybody?

They say the same thing in two registers. M vs Everybody is the shorthand - shorter on the chest, faster to read. Michigan v Everybody spells it out and reads more like a fixture. Both are lettering designs: no faces, no likenesses.

What sizes do the Vs Everybody shirts come in?

Apparel size ranges vary by design (S to 5XL overall) and each product page lists its exact sizes. Measure a shirt you own flat across the chest and match the size guide; between sizes, go up one.

Everything is printed after you order it. Delivery is an estimate: 5-12 business days in the US, with longer international transit possible; arrival before a specific game is not guaranteed.
