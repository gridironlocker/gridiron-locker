Custom T Shirts for Football Fans

Custom t shirts for football fans: your phrase, your colors, your game day. Send the idea and we turn it into an original football graphic - one you can buy a single copy of, printed on demand and shipped with tracking. It starts as a free inquiry with a proof and a price before anything is made.

How a custom shirt happens

01 - Your idea. A phrase, a nickname, a family motto, a group slogan or a running joke, plus the team and the colors.

02 - Our design. We turn the brief into an original football graphic in the same hand-drawn spirit as the shop, and send a proof with a price.

03 - Your shirt. Approve both and it prints on demand - tees, hoodies, sweatshirts, long sleeves, mugs or beanies, one at a time, no minimum.

What makes a great brief

The strongest requests name the exact words you want on the shirt and who they are for. "DAWG POUND FOREVER in orange and brown" beats "something for a Browns fan" every time - but the story behind the words matters too: the best custom designs come from jokes only one group of people would get.

Custom design FAQ

How much does a custom t shirt cost?

The inquiry is free. Pricing depends on the garment and the scope of the design, so we reply with a proof and a price before anything is made - you approve both before there is any order.

Can I order just one custom shirt?

Yes. Custom designs are printed on demand, so there is no minimum order - one shirt is a normal order, and the same file can go on hoodies, sweatshirts, mugs or beanies later.

How long does a custom design take?

We usually reply by email within 1–2 days with the first proof. After you approve it, printing is made to order and ships with tracking - allow production plus shipping time for delivery.

What should I put in my request?

The phrase or idea is the heart of it, then the team or theme, your colors and anything behind the story - a family motto, a group slogan, a running joke. The more specific the brief, the closer the first proof lands.

Is custom work officially licensed team merchandise?

No - and nothing we make is. Custom designs are original artwork in the same fan-made style as the shop: names and cities as text, never official marks. The trademark notice explains the stance.
