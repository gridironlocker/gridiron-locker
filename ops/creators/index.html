One link per creator. Every creator gets a single permanent destination (e.g. /michigan/joe/). New designs are added to the curation without ever changing the link the creator posts from social media.

Commission basis — Joe (JOE)

20% of net product sales generated through Joe's dedicated collection/page, excluding: taxes · shipping · refunds · cancellations · chargebacks.

Reconcile from Viralstyle order data: take the attributed order's product subtotal, subtract the excluded items above, and apply 20%. Payment terms: per the signed partnership agreement.

Customer-facing rule: the page communicates the collaboration (“Joe × Gridiron Locker”) and nothing more. The rate is never advertised — the guardrail test test_no_commission_terms_on_customer_page fails the build if “commission”, “20%” or affiliate wording reaches the public page.

Attribution stack (how orders become Joe-attributed)

LayerMechanismNotes
1 · Permanent referral URL /michigan/joe/ The only link Joe needs. Lives in the header/footer of the Michigan collection page.
2 · Referral param ?creator=JOE on every internal product link from the page Every product card and featured tile deep-links to the product page carrying the param.
3 · Persistent cookie gl_creator=JOE (90-day sliding TTL, path=/, SameSite=Lax) Set on first touch of ?creator=JOE; refreshed on every subsequent touch. Keeps attribution alive across direct visits, search, bookmarks and deep links.
4 · Checkout tagging creator=JOE&utm_source=creator&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=creator-JOE appended to the Viralstyle hand-off URL at click time Applied to a[href*="viralstyle.com"] while the cookie is present, so the order URL itself carries the attribution even when the buyer returns days later.
5 · GA4 events creator_attribution_set (cookie written), creator_page_view (on the creator page), creator_session (other pages under the cookie), shop_now_click with a creator dimension Splits attributed vs organic hand-offs; shop_now_click.creator=JOE is the funnel metric for commission reporting.

Priority when reconciling: checkout URL param (layer 4) > GA4 event (layer 5) > cookie window (layer 3). A missing checkout param still leaves the GA4 trail.

Workflow

  • Add a design to Joe's locker: append the slug to picks (and featured for the top row) in data/creators.json, then rebuild. Stale slugs skip silently.
  • Add a new creator: new record in creators.json with its own page_slug (e.g. michigan/<name>); the build generates the page, footer link, feed item, llms.txt entry and Michigan cross-link automatically.
  • Delisting a design: as usual via data/delisted.json — the curation filter drops it from the locker on the next build.
