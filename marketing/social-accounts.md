## 1. Second-choice handle (top pick)

**@gridironlocker.shop**

- Keeps the full brand name intact (search value), and adds the word **shop**,
  which is a buyer-intent keyword. When someone searches "gridiron locker",
  this handle still surfaces because the exact brand string is preserved.
- Reads professional on every platform and won't clash with the first account.

### If that is taken, pick the next one down the list — keep the same handle on EVERY platform

Use the *same* name on all platforms so searches for one platform find the others.
Only drop to the next row when a platform already holds that exact name.

| # | Handle | Why it ranks / reads well |
|---|--------|---------------------------|
| 1 | `@gridironlocker.shop` | Brand + buyer keyword "shop" |
| 2 | `@shopgridironlocker` | Same keyword up front, reads as a call to action |
| 3 | `@gridironlockergear` | Brand + product keyword "gear" (people search "football gear") |
| 4 | `@gridironlocker.apparel` | Brand + high-volume product keyword "apparel" |
| 5 | `@thegridironlocker` | "The" makes it feel official when the bare name is taken |
| 6 | `@officialgridironlocker` | Trust signal; strong on Facebook and TikTok |
| 7 | `@gridironlockerco` | Clean, short brand suffix |
| 8 | `@gridironlockerfans` | Adds the community keyword "fans" |
| 9 | `@weargridironlocker` | Action verb, punchy on Instagram and TikTok |
| 10 | `@gridiron.locker` / `@gridiron_locker` | Punctuation variant, closest to the original |

Notes:
- Skip numbers, random characters, or county codes (`_usa`, `123`) — they read as
  throwaway accounts and dilute search.
- Keep it under 15 characters when possible; shorter handles are easier to find,
  tag, and remember.
- Availability is checked in-app only (Instagram/TikTok/Facebook/Pinterest/YouTube
  require login to confirm). Work down the list in order.

## 2. Bios (no emojis) — FINAL, mapped per platform

Final wording, ampersand written as "&" (never "&amp;" on social platforms).

### Primary bio — Instagram, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest (130 chars)

> NFL & college football fan apparel. Cowboys, Browns Dawg Pound, Packers & Michigan gear. Custom apparel available. Built for fans.

### Compact alternate (114 chars)

> NFL & college football fan gear. Cowboys, Browns, Packers & Michigan. Custom apparel available. Designed for fans.

### TikTok (must be under 80 chars — 71 chars)

> Cowboys, Browns, Packers & Michigan fan gear. Custom apparel available.

### Long / SEO version — YouTube, Pinterest About, Facebook About (183 chars)

> Football fan apparel, tees & hoodies for Dallas Cowboys, Cleveland Browns Dawg Pound, Green Bay Packers & Michigan fans. Custom apparel available. Designed by fans, built for Sundays.

### Hashtags — use in captions and posts, not the bio

> #NFL #Football #NFLApparel #FootballApparel #DallasCowboys #Browns #DawgPound #Packers #Michigan

Why not in the bio: the tag line alone is 96 chars. On Instagram it would push
the bio past the 150-char limit, and TikTok's 80-char limit leaves no room at
all. Paste the full hashtag set into post captions instead (or into the
YouTube / Pinterest profiles, which have space to spare).
