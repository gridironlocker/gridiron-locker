  verticals — content about players as *people*, not just athletes.
  [4](https://digiday.com/media/inside-the-nfls-youth-focused-social-strategy/)

### Sports brands on TikTok/Instagram (playbook patterns)
- **Emotional hooks + series** ("Countdown to the Big Match", "Workout
  Wednesdays") and **UGC challenges** (#MyDailyRun) — fans make the content,
  brand reposts it. [5](https://picklesocial.com/insights/how-sports-brands-lead-on-tiktok-and-instagram)
- 2026 POD viral rules: **7-second hook** (visual must change in the first
  second), "wait-what" reveal (blank → finished print), "wrong answer", and
  "POV: you found the niche nobody prints" frames; designs that **read in 2
  seconds** (bold typography, two-color minimalism); 3-layer hashtag stack;
  pin the **specific product**, not the whole store.
  [6](https://inkedjoy.com/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-tiktok-for-print-on-demand-seller)

### TikTok football fashion (the female/outfit angle rivals underuse)
- Massive trend surface: **jersey reworks** (scrunch/ruched jersey, jersey →
  puffer, jersey → mini dress), "**nfl baddie**" outfits, **wearing-vs-styling**,
  GRWM/OOTD game-day fits. [7](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/nfl-fashion)
  [8](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/nfl-baddie-outfits)

### The gap (where we beat them)
1. **Rivals post flat product mockups.** We run fashion-editorial, on-model
   campaign shots — chest-down framing, styled layers, film grain, cinematic
   grade. Visually we out-class a flat-lay 10 out of 10 times.
2. **Rivals wait for a moment to happen.** We already have a **trend-scoring
   engine** (134 designs ranked against live search terms + repeated headline

3. **Rivals ignore the female fashion angle in this niche.** The NFL's own data

4. **Rivals sell one moment.** We sell the *locker*: 134 designs across four
