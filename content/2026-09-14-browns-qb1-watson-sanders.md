### 0.2 HEADLINE → SIGNAL → TREND → OPPORTUNITY (the distinction that matters)

- **HEADLINE:** "Jaguars 34, Browns 10." (a scoreline, not a trend)
- **SIGNAL:** Watson named the Week 2 starter on Monday morning by the head coach, hours after national
  insiders said the job was open. Adam Schefter on *Get Up* (Sep 14). The internal Fan Trend Index shows
  **Deshaun Watson at 50 headline mentions in a 10-day window (FTI 100, rank #1)** — the hottest name on
  the board across all four of the brand's fanbases. Shedeur Sanders 12 (FTI 24), Todd Monken 8 (FTI 16).
- **TREND (supported, and this is the honest version):** the argument is no longer *"did we lose"*, it is
  *"is this the last patience we're offering."* It crossed from fandom to national analysis inside ~18 hours
  (a *New York Times* piece published within the window asks how long Cleveland will absorb the cost of the
  Watson era). That is the pattern of a real trend: local beat → national column → network show → fan
  re-litigation of an older, settled-feeling decision.
- **NOT claimed:** "trending on social." The trend engine's fan connectors returned **0 fan posts** today
  (Reddit HTTP 403 blocked; X and Meta tokens not configured). So we have **press-volume evidence, not
  social-volume evidence**, and the article is written to that standard. Every "fans are saying" line in the
  piece is anchored to a named, dated outlet, not to an invented thread.
- **OPPORTUNITY:** nobody is serving the fan who is not a Browns beat follower and just wants the plain
  answer: who is playing Sunday, why, and what happens if it goes wrong again.

## 2. Article

*(Full body copy is in `2026-09-14-browns-qb1-watson-sanders-wordpress.html` — the WordPress-ready version.
Plain-text reading copy below for editorial review.)*

**Intro**

Six hundred and ninety-three days. That's how long Deshaun Watson went between NFL starts — one torn
Achilles in October 2024, another during rehab, a lost 2025, then a training-camp win over the player half
of Cleveland actually wanted to watch. His return lasted about a quarter and a half. The Browns were beaten
34-10 in Jacksonville on Sunday, and by Monday morning the only thing anyone in Cleveland was still
arguing about wasn't the score. It was the quarterback.

**Quick Answer**

Todd Monken said on Monday, September 14 that he anticipates Watson starting Week 2 at Tampa Bay on
Sunday, September 20 (1:00 PM ET, CBS). It was not framed as a long-term coronation. ESPN's Adam Schefter
reported the original plan was to run Watson for the first two games — both on the road — then re-evaluate.
The pressure point is the home opener on Sunday, September 27 against Carolina, the Browns' first home
game after the latest home opener in franchise scheduling history since 1975.

*(Full four-bullet version in the WordPress file.)*

**Body sections**

1. `What Actually Happened in Jacksonville` — the score, the opening-drive pick, the 24-0 hole, the
   stat line, the 46-yard TD to rookie Denzel Boston in garbage time, and the 11 penalties.
2. `Why Monken Won't Put This on the Quarterback Alone` — his quote, and the three structural facts
   (protection, penalties, contract mechanics) that make a change more complicated than a hot take.
3. **Watson vs Sanders table** — 2025 stats, contract, what each gives the offense. (Snippet + AEO bait.)
4. `The Case For Shedeur Sanders, In Plain Numbers` — his 2025 finish, the 57% completion problem,
   why the coach hesitates.
5. `The Fan Argument, Accurately Described` — boos in preseason, Watson calling them disrespectful,
   NYT/Pluto/Cabot, and what the *sentiment* actually is (anxious, not hostile).
6. `The Calendar: Every Date That Changes This Story` — Sep 20 Tampa, Sep 27 Carolina, Oct 1 Steelers on TNF.
7. `Shop the Look` — the gear section, see below.

**Fan perspective** (kept honest — sourced, not invented): the internal Fan Trend Index logged Watson at
50 headline mentions in a 10-day window — #1 across Browns, Packers, Michigan and Cowboys combined.
That is the number that says this is the conversation, not a vibe.

The article also states plainly that there is **no Shedeur Sanders shirt in the locker** — honesty that
doubles as a custom-design funnel.
