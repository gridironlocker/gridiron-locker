## Read-outs
1. **5 of 6 rising terms are brand queries.** We cannot bid on or optimize for other
   companies' trademarks (standing rule + trademark notice) — treat them purely as
   demand weather. The one generic term ("custom t shirts") is now served by a
   dedicated landing page wired into nav/footer/popup.
2. **Sweatshirt & hoodie category is the accelerating garment** (comfrt +21.7%,
   parke +18.7%) — reinforces two standing gaps: we have **1 hoodie in 95 products**
   and zero seasonal sweatshirts. The design queue (hoodie variants of top sellers,
   seasonal Q4) is the supply-side answer; needs Mayzing uploads (owner action).
3. **Timing:** the window covers the pre-holiday ramp; category growth should keep
   climbing through Black Friday. Design/upload decisions made after ~Nov 20 miss
   the Q4 shipping window.
4. Follow-up measurement: next Merchant trends export + weekly report
   (`python3 ops/weekly_growth_report.py`) to see whether "custom t shirts" growth
   continues and whether our /custom-t-shirts/ page picks up impressions.
